Sunshine Coast Water Taxi

Eastbourne Express — the Sunday boat home

Eastbourne (Keats Island) → Horseshoe Bay, direct. About 30 minutes on the water.

Just checking how this works?

Pick your Sunday and how many of you are travelling. Then pick the part of the day you'd like to leave — morning, midday, or afternoon — rather than an exact time.

We set the exact boat time once we know how many people want each window, and text it to you by 6 pm Saturday. TBC: confirm-by time

Nothing is charged until your boat is confirmed.

What does it cost?
How do we all get the $30 fare?

Fill the boat. A boat has 12 seats; once 10 or more people are booked on it, the fare drops to $30 for everyone on it. Fuel and the booking fee stay the same. TBC: 10 or 12?

The easiest way is to get your neighbours on the same window. You'll see how close each window is to the $30 fare when you pick one, and your card is only charged the final price once the boat is confirmed.

Will the boat actually run?

A boat runs when about 7 people book the same window. You'll see how close each window is to running before you choose, and if yours hasn't reached 7 by Thursday 6 pm TBC, we'll text you your options.

If your window doesn't fill, we'll offer you the nearest boat that is running, or let your spot go at no charge. Your call.

A guess is fine. A weekend bag is about 20 lb, a full cooler of food about 50, a 24 of beer about 20. Dogs don't count.

Who counts as a passenger?

Everyone who takes a seat, including kids. Dogs, bikes and bags don't count, but the boat holds 12 people plus their stuff, so give us a rough weight for your gear above.

More than 12 of you? Give us a call and we'll sort a second boat.

Step 2

When would you like to leave?

Pick the part of the day. We set the exact boat time once we know who's coming.

When will I know my boat time?

By 6 pm Saturday TBC, by text. Most people want the last boat, so we set its time first and work back from there: the more of you in a window, the more likely it runs exactly when you'd like.

Each window shows how many are booked and how many more it needs. A boat runs at 7.

Why does it show dogs?

Dogs ride free, and that isn't changing. But some boats end up with a lot of them, and not every dog enjoys the company. Each window shows how many dogs are booked so far. If yours would rather a quieter ride, pick a window with fewer.

Which window gets us the $30 fare?

Whichever one you can fill. The bar on each window shows the mark for the $30 fare. Pick the window your neighbours are on, or bring them to yours.

Step 3

Happy to take another boat if it helps one fill?

Your first pick stays your first pick. Tick anything else that would also work.

Why would I tick a backup?

Boats run when enough people want the same window. If yours is one short and the next one over is running, a backup gets you home instead of a refund.

It's also how boats fill — and a full boat is how everyone on it gets the $30 fare.

Tick nothing and we take that as "only this window works for me". That's fine too. Your first pick is never moved to make room for someone else — seats go to the first 12 who book.

What if more people want my window than fit on one boat?

The first 12 to book have the seats. After that you can join the waitlist: you'll see your place in line, and you're next up if someone cancels. If enough people are waiting, a second boat may be added — that's the skipper's call, not a promise.

Tick a backup so waiting isn't your only way home.

Step 4

Who's the lead passenger?

We text the boat time and any changes to this number. No app, no account.

Texts about this booking come either way. Reply STOP to the extras any time.

Booking for someone else?

Put the traveller's name and mobile here, so the boat texts reach the person on the dock. You can still pay with your own card at the next step.
